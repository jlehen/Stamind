# My Weekly Structure

> **AUTHORITY: PRESCRIPTIVE.** This is the athlete's own weekly template. Where it
> and `sustainable_training.md` disagree on a session count, this file wins.

Strength is a year-round priority in its own right, not support for the current goal.

- **2/week Resistance Training** in every phase, including taper, race week and
  off-season. This replaces the "1/week Resistance Training" line of every block in
  `sustainable_training.md`.
- Trim their volume when the endurance block demands it. Never drop one entirely; the
  only reason to drop a strength session is a constraint that makes the week impossible
  to lay out properly.
- Everything else (the high-intensity count per block, the taper rules, the block order)
  follows `sustainable_training.md` unchanged.
